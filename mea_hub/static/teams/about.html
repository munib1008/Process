MEA Process Hub

Internal tool for process documentation: process library, flowcharts, review and approval, exports.

Where the data is

The tool runs on a server of your organisation. Process documents, flowcharts, user names, roles, comments and the audit log are stored in that server's data folder. The Teams tab only displays the tool; the tool does not send this content to Microsoft Teams or to any other service.

Sign-in

The tool has its own user names and passwords, managed by its administrator. It does not use your Microsoft 365 sign-in.

Rules of use and contact

Use of this tool and of the documents in it follows your organisation's policies for internal information. For access, corrections or questions contact the administrator of MEA Process Hub in your organisation.
